Privacy

Your data

What this site keeps about you, why, and how to have it removed.

Who we are

Niwaki, the project behind niwakiai.com. For anything about your data, write to niwakiai@gmail.com.

What we keep, and why

If you join the waiting list: your email address and name, your company and role if you give them, and when you agreed and to which version of this notice. We use them only to write to you about Kanji: early access, its release and news about it. We keep them on your consent (GDPR, Article 6(1)(a)), which you can withdraw at any time; withdrawing doesn’t affect what was done before. Giving them is up to you, but without an email address we can’t write to you.

When you visit: the site sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser. Cloudflare Web Analytics counts visits without cookies and without following you across sites. GitHub Pages, which serves the site, may log visitors’ IP addresses for security, as web servers do.

Who holds it

Cloudflare, Inc. stores the waiting list for us and processes it only on our instructions, under its data processing terms. GitHub, Inc. hosts the site. Both are based in the United States and receive data from the EU under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework. We don’t sell your details or share them with anyone else, and we make no automated decisions about you.

How long we keep it

Until you withdraw your consent or ask us to delete it. We review the list once a year and delete what we no longer need.

Your rights

You can ask to see your details, correct them, delete them, restrict or object to their use, or receive them in a portable form, and you can withdraw your consent at any time: write to niwakiai@gmail.com and we’ll answer within a month. You can also complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (aepd.es) or the authority in your own country.
